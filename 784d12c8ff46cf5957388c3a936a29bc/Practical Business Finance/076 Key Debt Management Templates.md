# Key Debt Management Templates

_Type: ebook · Section: Practical Business Finance_

## Debt Management

Managing debt effectively is a crucial step toward financial stability and peace of mind. Whether you're juggling multiple accounts or want a clearer view of your repayment strategy, these two downloadable templates help (portal attachments):

- **Debt Reduction Calculator Template:** uses the Snowball and Avalanche methods to help you prioritise repayments and see your path to being debt-free.
- **Debt Management Template:** a breakdown of account balances across ageing periods, for tracking overdue payments and planning follow-ups.
