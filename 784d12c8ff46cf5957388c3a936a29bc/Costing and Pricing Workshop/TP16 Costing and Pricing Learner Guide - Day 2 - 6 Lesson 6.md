LESSON SIX:

TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF SALES

Your  terms  &  conditions  of  sale  set  out  the  parameters  and  requirements  under  which  your  business  will  engage  in
transactions with potential or existing customers.

They should be seen as a binding and legal agreement between yourself and the customer in terms of your engagement
and interaction. Thus, they need to be communicated and agreed to clearly and up front before any work commences.
They should include:

•

The prices of the goods or the services that have been purchased.

•

The mode of payment for the goods or services purchased.

•  Details and feature specifications of the goods or services.

•

The details about the delivery date for the goods or services.

•

The  responsibilities  of  the  customer  according  to  the  terms  and  conditions  as  decided  upon  by  the  seller  and  the
customer.

•

The responsibilities of the supplier as decided upon by the seller and the customer.

•

The limit on the liabilities of the supplier, in case of any problem.

•

The details regarding the schedule of the proposal.

•

•

In case the deal is between a business and a client (B2C), then details about the clause regarding cancellation of the
purchase order and refund of money needs to be included.

In  case  of  a  deal  between  two  business  parties  (B2B),  there  will  be  clauses  regarding  alterations,  warranty,
compensation, intellectual property rights, and termination of the deal.
