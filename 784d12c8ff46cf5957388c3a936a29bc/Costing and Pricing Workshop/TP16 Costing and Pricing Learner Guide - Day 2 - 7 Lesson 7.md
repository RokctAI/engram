LESSON SEVEN:
CREATING EFFECTIVE PRICELISTS & SALES PROPOSALS
Remember we said at the outset that successful selling is about making it as easy as possible to do business with you.
This starts with having a clear, concise and attractive pricelist or proposal that not only gives your potential customer all
the information they need to make a decision as to whether to buy or not, but also encourages them to do so.
What makes an effective Price List, Proposal or Catalog?
1. It’s clear, comprehensive and easy to use
Your price list, fee structure or catalog may very well be the most widely used document you produce. It’s a constant
source of reference for your customers. A well-designed, easy-to-use document makes it easier for them to do business with
you. It also illustrates your professionalism.
2. It complements your other marketing efforts
A good price list or catalog is a single source for all your product/service information. It should complement your other
marketing efforts, such as websites, brochures etc.
3. It makes the buying decision easy
It contains all the information buyers need and it makes it easy for them to find that information. Good layout, effective
descriptions, codes, quick references, relevant images and graphics and sales terms & conditions help to avoid user
frustration.
4. It’s cost-effective
Good design also contributes to lower production costs by making efficient use of space and keeping page counts to a
minimum. Save those trees!
5. It’s available in a variety of media
While there will always be a need for some hard copy price lists, the era of digital pricing and product information has
arrived. You need to be able to distribute this data on CDs, DVDs, by e-mail broadcast, or over the Web.
Basic Structure
The style of pricelists may differ markedly, but certain key elements are common in most of them:
Intro – some background information on your company, philosophy, products, awards & accolades, services and offerings
etc. Keep it concise (maximum one page). It is often a good idea to include an image or two.
Main Body – your product and pricing information including code, price, minimum quantities, colours and a reference
picture if possible for products, and full breakdowns of your training, workshops and other offerings if you sell services.
Sales Terms & Conditions – all your terms and conditions of sale including payment terms, freight & delivery etc.

BUILDING YOUR OWN PRICELIST IN WORD
MS Word is a user-friendly and comprehensive programme that allows you to build a pricelist that includes all the
information a customer needs in order to make a buying decision, including codes, descriptions, product dimensions,
minimum quantities, price and a reference picture.
To build a pricelist in Word, follow these simple steps:
1. Open a blank Word document
2. Starting at the top, insert your letterhead if you have one – Do this by going to the Insert menu, and clicking on
Header& Footer, then select Header and insert your letterhead or company logo
3. Next click Footer, and fill in your contact details
4. You can choose to insert an image of your company, company premises or employees next by following the same
procedure but selecting Insert Picture instead

5. Once this is completed, write a short profile of your company including your operating philosophy, USP’s (Unique
Selling Points) etc.
6. Once done, go onto the next page
7. Next, go to the Insert menu on your word toolbar, click Table and insert a table with 5 columns (6 if you want to
include colour options)
8. Fill in the relevant headings at the top of the pricelist, and as many lines as you require to showcase your range/s –
these headings are Product Code/Name & Description/Minimum Order Quantity/Price/Reference Picture
9. Fill in all the relevant details for the different products
10. Once done, go to the last column (reference Picture) and click Insert/Picture. Find the correct picture and click Insert
11. Do this for all the images required. You may need to click and drag on some of their borders to make them fit the
space properly.
12. Once this is completed, go to a new page and write your sales terms & conditions. You can also insert another image
or images here if you wish.
13. Finally, double click on one of the images in the document and select
Compress. When the programme asks if you want to do this for all
images in the document, click yes.
