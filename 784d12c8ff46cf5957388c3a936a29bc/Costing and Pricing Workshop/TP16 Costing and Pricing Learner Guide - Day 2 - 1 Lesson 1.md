LESSON ONE:

THE RIGHT TOOLS FOR THE JOB

A primary secret to success in business, no matter what your industry, product or service might be, is to make it as easy as
possible for your customers to do business with you. The easier and more enjoyable it is for them to buy from or support
you, the more they will!

This sounds like an obvious statement, but it is surprising how many companies make it difficult for our customers. We
are hard to get hold of, we have little or no online presence, we do not communicate efficiently, our pricing structure is
confusing, or we do not have the necessary sales tools available to make things clear.

•  A clear, concise and consistent pricing structure – that accommodates all your potential customers - and clear sales
terms & conditions, are absolute fundamentals in ANY business. They are an indication to the potential customer that
yours is a professional, organized and competent company, one that they can confidently do business with.

•  The same applies to your sales tools such as pricelists, catalogues and company profiles. They need to be professional

and easy to understand, with all the info a potential client needs to make a buying decision.

Today  we  will  delve  into  the  tricky  subject  of  pricing  –  we  will  look  at  pricing  methodologies,  pricing  levels,  pricing
strategies and developing effective price lists – including sales terms and conditions and other relevant information. We
will cover this information from several angles, and address specifics for both product and service-based businesses.

Remember that the mark-ups, tools and templates shared here are examples only – every business and every sector is
different, and so the challenge for you is to adapt this information to the needs of your own organisation.

Also remember that even if you offer a service, building something tangible, professional and compelling to share with
your client relating to pricing will put you ahead of the pack.

Have fun!
