FINANCIAL ASSESSMENT

Here are some of the questions you need to ask yourself to make sure you have covered all the financial bases in running
your organization:

1.  Do you know what the total of your overheads, or running costs, will be for the next 12 months?

	 Yes, and they’re itemised in our business plan/financial projections/budget
	 Not really
	 I am not sure how to work them out

2.  Do you have an idea of how much you can expect to pay out in direct costs (direct transport, labour & materials) for

the next 12 months?
	 Yes (or I don’t have any direct costs)
	 No
	 I am not sure how to work them out

3.  Have you set monthly Sales Targets based on your company’s financial needs and do you monitor them?

	 Yes
	 Not really, but I’m not sure I have to because things are ok
	 I wouldn’t know how to establish such targets

4.  Have you made an estimate of your net profit (after expenses) for the next 12 months, and is it enough to sustain the

business financially?
	 Yes
	 No, but I have made it this far so I think things are ok
	 I wouldn’t know how to

5.  Are you able to live on the income you make from your business?

	 Yes, and I have worked out a ‘Survival Budget” for the slower months
	 No, but we have orders pending so I am confident it will improve
	 I just take out some cash when I can
	 No, but my partner/wife/husband is working elsewhere so it is manageable

6.  Have  you  given  any  thought  to  the  main  risks  your  business  could  face  financially?  Do  you  have  a  Plan  B  for

unforeseen eventualities?
	 I have carried out a financial SWOT analysis and have planned for all contingencies
	 I believe we will be fine if we work hard and stick to our strategy
	 I haven’t carried out any type of risk assessment

7.  Are you confident that the prices you sell your product or services at are correct?

	 I am not sure, but people keep buying from me so it seems to be working so far
	 Yes, and I have got a reasonable margin to play with, if necessary

8.  Have  you  included  all  the  costs  involved  in  the  delivery  of  your  product  or  service  in  your  costing  and  are  you

confident you have covered every base?
	 No, I am not confident that all my costs are factored in
	 Yes – I have included every last cent into my costing process
	 I am not sure how to work this stuff out

9.  Have you calculated your ‘Breakeven’ point and looked at ways to make your business safer, financially?

	 No, I am just going flat out until I make it - or go bust
	 Yes - and I’m aware of the areas to address in case of problems
	 I’ve had enough of this f*%$# test, let me out!
