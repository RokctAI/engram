Money Belief Systems: Pre-Workshop Instructions

1.  Complete the Klontz Money Script® Inventory-Revised (KMSI-R)

  https://www.bradklontz.com/moneyscriptstest

  This will take about 10 minutes

  At the end of the questionnaire there are questions about salary, net worth and other

investment-type questions. Just put any answers here.

  You will be emailed the report

2.  Reflect on how accurate the results are for you

3.  Given that our money beliefs are formed during our early years, reflect on your

financial situation during your growing up years:

  What would you say the “Money Scripts” were (and maybe still are) of your parents or

primary caregivers you grew up with?

  Do you feel any of your current money beliefs have any links back to your growing up

years?

4.  “Finance Overwhelm / Anxiety” as a special case contributor to Money Beliefs

  People can develop strong negative emotions around Finance and Accounting, leading to

degrees of mental “shutdown” and avoidance

  For Entrepreneurs this can be particularly troublesome, especially in the early years, when
there aren’t sufficient resources to pay for regular professional assistance (even when
there are resources, it is still the responsibility of the Entrepreneur to be abreast of, and
critically engage with, the Finances of their business)

  Reflect on your own situation:

a.  What do you feel could explain this personally, ans as a global phenomenon?

b.  Do you love doing Finance and Accounting admin in your business, or are you on the

other end of the scale?

c.  If you struggle with the Financial aspects of your business; what practical strategies

would help you overcome this?
