### The Importance of Culture

Leadership, core values, behaviours and ethics all feed into your company culture.

In essence, company culture is the personality of a company. It defines the environment in which employees work. Company culture includes a variety of elements, including work environment, company mission, value, ethics, expectations, and goals.

As the world becomes more connected through technology, businesses need to work so much harder to gain a sustainable competitive advantage. In the past, creating a great product, coupled with excellent service was enough to keep you at the top of your game.

However, the marketplace has changed, and conditions have become more volatile. Competitors are constantly developing new innovations and can easily imitate your products or services. Technology is evolving at a rapid pace. Customers are demanding more responsive and personalised service. Employee values and expectations are shifting, with Millennials now comprising the largest generational part in the workforce.

One competitive advantage you can leverage consistently—a differentiator that can’t duplicated—is your company culture. Your culture is unique to your organisation, helping to shape your company’s brand identity, improve employee retention, and inspire and motivate your people.
