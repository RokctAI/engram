| Success Wheel Element | What it Means | Why it is Important |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5. Sales & Marketing** | How effective are your processes in terms of managing the sales function in your business? Do you have an effective online presence? Is your team sufficiently skilled to manage & grow the sales & marketing function in your business? | Without sales, you have no business, whether you are selling cars, cabbages or a service. You need to ensure that your sales team has all the tools they require (price lists, catalogues, samples) and that your organisation has an appropriate marketing presence to create the necessary awareness amongst your target market/s. |
| **6. Operational & Quality Management Systems (QMS)** | Are you producing your product or delivering your service in the most efficient way possible? How do you ensure consistent delivery and maintenance of quality standards, not just for your products but for everything the business does, or wants to do? | Smooth operations do not happen by accident, they are the result of sound forethought and planning for the future. Operational efficiency can save you time, save you money and minimise losses due to quality control issues, wastage, shrinkage or unavailability of crucial resources such as materials or staff. |
| **7. Product or Service** | Is your product or service properly aligned to the target market/s you have identified? Is it correctly priced? What about packaging and branding? What elements are and are not included in your offering, and are your clients aware of this? | Successful organisations thrive because either they offer something unique, or they offer a similar product or service to the competition but do so more efficiently, cheaper and with a greater focus on customer service. If your product or service is generic and easy to copy, it will be copied. Make sure you stay innovative and open to new possibilities in terms of range extension. |
| **8. Financial Sustainability & Growth** | Do you have an effective system for recording, tracking and analyzing your financial performance? Do you get the reports you need, when you need them to help you manage this side of the business? How profitable and viable is your business at the moment? | The most important element of success is also the most frequently overlooked or poorly managed in an organisation. A well-managed financial programme is not just important from a day-to-day operational perspective, but makes applying for finance, attracting new investors or funders and planning for the future so much easier. |
| **9. Social & Environmental** | Are you involved in any social or environmental initiatives? How does your business ‘give back’, and is this a part of your strategy and operations or just ad hoc gestures as and when possible? Are you working in an environmentally friendly way? | The more pressure we put on our environment, the more important it becomes to work in a sustainable way – not just because it is the right thing to do, but because it increasingly makes business sense. |
