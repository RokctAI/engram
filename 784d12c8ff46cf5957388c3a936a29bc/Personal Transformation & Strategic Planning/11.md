## 3. Principle #3 – Life is for Learning

Success in business requires an attitude of continual learning and development. Every successful entrepreneur has faced challenge and hardship, made wrong decisions and tasted failure – the key to success is how you turn these challenges and failures into lessons to make you stronger and more experienced.
