## 12. Principle #11 – Focus is key to success

If you have big dreams to expand your business that you wish to achieve, you will need to focus and prioritise if you are to achieve them in this lifetime.

In his book *“Mastering the Rockefeller Habits: What You Must Do to Increase the Value of Your Growing Firm”*, Verne Harnish explores 10 habits that have not changed for over 100 years since John D. Rockefeller implemented them in his business — the result of which made him the wealthiest man ever, even by today’s standards.

The 10 Rockefeller Habits provides business leaders with a clear way to make sure communication is clear and that everyone knows about the vision and strategy and where they play a role.

> ### Rockefeller Habit #2
> **Everyone is aligned with the #1 thing that needs to be accomplished this quarter to move the company forward**

A powerful way to achieve/implement your strategy is to break your company goals into smaller chunks – perhaps setting a few goals for this year and setting only one or two goals for the next 90 days. Avoid the temptation of listing multiple ‘90-day goals’ as this will create confusion and loss of focus, leading to failure. Make sure that everyone in your team knows the goal and understands their contribution to the achievement thereof.

If you achieve one 90-day priority every quarter, your results over a lifetime will explode exponentially!
