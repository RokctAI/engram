## 8. Principle #8 - Focus on what is important

### Strategic Objectives

This is where the ‘rubber hits the road’ in strategic planning – and where we identify the specific actions, we need to take to generate success.

### Determine What is Important

Determining your Strategic Objectives is about deciding what is truly important to the success of your business. To do this we go through the Five Pillars one at a time and identify what we need to do to satisfy these Pillars.

For example, if your People Pillar is to employ, train and develop the best people for the job, what actually needs to be done in your business to make this happen?

### Developing Your Strategic Objectives

Every business is different, so every business will have a different set of Strategic Objectives, however we have given you some examples to help guide you as you decide on yours:

#### **Pillar 1: People**
*   **Strategic Objectives:**
    1.  Improve in-house staff wellness programme
    2.  Get project management certification
    3.  Develop a recruitment and staff retention plan

#### **Pillar 2: Systems and Processes**
*   **Strategic Objectives:**
    1.  Improve Quality Management and reduce comebacks
    2.  Improve overall Policies and Procedures
    3.  Implement a Customer Relationship Management System

#### **Pillar 3: Finance & Governance**
*   **Strategic Objectives:**
    1.  Improve BEE Rating from Level 3 to Level 1
    2.  Reduce overheads by 10%
    3.  Implement a financial management system

#### **Pillar 4: Customers**
*   **Strategic Objectives:**
    1.  Expand into one new territory
    2.  More closely align pricing & products to target market
    3.  Become SETA certified

#### **Pillar 5: Social and Environmental**
*   **Strategic Objectives:**
    1.  Implement recycling process
    2.  Have BEE policy in place
    3.  Ensure waste reduction practices in place
