All nine elements of the Fetola Success Wheel (and self-assessment), fall into one of these Five Pillars, as follows:

| Pillar | Success Wheel |
| :--- | :--- |
| **People** | 1. Human Resource<br>2. Leadership & Change Potential |
| **Systems & Processes** | 3. Operational & Quality Management Systems<br>4. Technology & Communications |
| **Finance and Governance** | 5. Financial Sustainability & Growth<br>6. Legislation & Compliance |
| **Customers** | 7. Sales & Marketing<br>8. Product or Service |
| **Social & Environmental** | 9. Social & community impact<br>10. Environmental impact |

I think you will all agree that the Five Pillars – People, Systems & Processes, Finance & Governance, Customers and Social & Environmental - are important, but perhaps it is worth investigating why this is the case in a bit more detail.
