Leaders are often described as visionary people, inspiring confidence and influencing others. But there are many different styles of leadership.

Being effective as a leader means understanding your style and staying true to it, especially in a small business where a leader can make or break success.

Finding your own voice comes first. The journey to be a truly great leader starts with understanding yourself....

### Leadership

Leadership is an essential skill for any successful entrepreneur - whether leading others or just yourself. In fact, no leader can effectively lead others if they are unable to lead themselves.

Leadership is not a role that can be assigned to somebody – it is a personal choice that is driven by an inner desire and long-term commitments.

A leader does not choose his or her followers – it is the followers who choose their leaders!
