*   **Paper flyers - make sure you indicate that these are recyclable:** If you must print flyers for your business, make sure you use gloss-free paper, and clearly print the phrase: “Do not litter” and “This flyer is recyclable” on the reverse. Though it may be obvious Gross Domestic Products (GDP) growth to some, may come as significant cost to others, many people need that reminder.
*   **Print in an environmentally-friendly way:** Make sure you’re printing in a way that has a minimal effect on the environment – reverse-sided, black and white. For internal office work use scrap paper.
*   **Use shredded scrap paper for shipping or packing:** When shipping breakable marketing materials (i.e., promotional mugs) use your scrap paper as packing materials – just be sure to run all scrap paper through a shredder first.

### Energy savings

The hikes in energy costs between 1 April 2013 and 31 March 2018 approved by NERSA represent an increase of at least 47% in the electricity costs for businesses over the five-year period.

Before spending money on energy efficient equipment for your business, you can implement simple tips and strategies that can easily cut costs by 20%. About 30% of the energy used in commercial and industrial buildings is used inefficiently or unnecessarily. If the energy efficiency of our commercial and industrial buildings was improved by 10%, the reduction in greenhouse gas emissions would be the equivalent of taking 30 million cars off the road. The largest energy consumer in office buildings is generally the heating, ventilation and air-conditioning (HVAC), as well as lighting. Here’s how you can save energy:

*   **Switch off:** Involve staff and increase awareness about the need to switch lights off and turn appliances off when not in use. Make this part of the company culture. Remind staff at meetings and place stickers above light switches. Turn appliances off at the wall plug, rather than leaving them on standby as this can still draw about 20% or more of normal electricity use (examples are TVs, music systems, computers, phone chargers etc). Also turn the geyser off when you go on holiday.
    *   *A photocopy machine left on overnight wastes enough electricity to print 16 000 copies.*
*   **Use only the light you need:** Light switches should be clearly labelled to help employees to select only those lights they need (for example, when cleaning or restocking the store after hours). Lights in unoccupied areas should be switched off.
    *   *Occupancy or daylight sensors are also useful – this way you don’t need to rely on people to remember to switch off. Sensors can achieve savings of up to 50% on lighting costs in some operations.*
