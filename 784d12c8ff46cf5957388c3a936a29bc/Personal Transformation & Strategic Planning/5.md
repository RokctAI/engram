## 2. Principle #2 – Know your strengths

Running a business is challenging on many levels. It does not matter if you grow the best vegetables in the world, or make the most durable bricks, or provide the most efficient printing and photocopying services, if you do not have solid business processes and systems supporting what you make, do or offer, your best efforts will be in vain.

A business is only as strong as its weakest link and often the areas that hold you back are the areas that you find challenging or even boring! It's for this reason that we work systematically to find where these are and remedy them.

There are nine key areas that are important to the growth of any business. You have already completed a Fetola Success Wheel Scorecard self-assessment, now let's take another look at these ten areas:

### Figure 1: Fetola Success Wheel©

The Fetola Success Wheel consists of five core pillars, each containing two key focus areas:

1.  **PEOPLE**
    *   Human Resources
    *   Leadership & Vision
2.  **SOCIAL & ENVIRONMENTAL**
    *   Job Creation and Poverty alleviation
    *   Environmental sustainability
3.  **CUSTOMERS**
    *   Sales & Marketing
    *   Product or Services
4.  **FINANCE & GOVERNANCE**
    *   Financial Sustainability & Growth
    *   Legislation & Compliance
5.  **SYSTEMS & PROCESSES**
    *   Technology & Communication
    *   Operational & QMS
