### Skills Development Levy (SDL)

*   If an employer believes that in the following 12-month period the remuneration payable to employees will exceed R500 000, the employer must register for the Skills Development Levy.
*   Those required to register for the Skills development Fund must register through SARS and indicate the jurisdiction of the SETA (Skills Education and Training Authority) within which the employer must be classified as well as the applicable SIC code.
*   Standard Industrial Classification (SIC) Codes are updated each year.
*   One-person businesses with no employees do not have to register for the SDL.
*   The Skills Development Levy constitutes a payment of 1% of the employee’s salary and is paid by the employer over to SARS with the EMP201 submission.
*   Up to 20% of the amount paid in respect of the Levy can be claimed back upon the successful submission of a Workplace Skills Plan (WSP) to your relevant SETA by 30th April each year for training to be conducted in the year to come.
*   An Annual Training Report (ATR) must also be submitted to the SETA latest 30th April for training conducted over the past year.
*   The following employers are exempt from paying the Skills Development Levy:
    *   The public service employer
    *   A public benefit organisation that is in possession of an exemption letter issued by SARS
    *   Public entities that get more than 80% of their money from Parliament
    *   Employers who do not have to register according to the Income Tax Act

### Workmen’s Compensation Fund

*   COID (Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases) is a government body that compensates workers who have been injured at work or who have contracted a disease. The relevant details are stipulated in the Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act.
*   Employees can claim in the event of Occupational Injuries and Diseases and in the case of an employee’s death their dependants can then claim.
*   An employer must register with COID within 7 days of employing their first employee.
*   Employers must fill in the W. As2 form. The form is available at any labour centre or on the Department of Labour website. The form must be mailed, along with a copy of the company registration form, to: PO Box 955 Pretoria 0001 or emailed to: **cfcallcentre@labour.gov.za** or **cfenquiries@labour.gov.za**
*   There are 2 return of earnings (ROE) disclosures that must be submitted annually. One will be the actual annual payroll of the business for the past financial year and the other for the projected annual payroll of the business for the upcoming financial year.
*   The amount payable to the Compensation Fund is dependent on the staff complement of your business and the industry that your business operates in.
*   Failure to submit the ROEs is against the law and penalties will be charged.
*   The ROE’s submission and renewal must be done during 01 April to 31st May annually.
*   The Act excludes:
    *   Workers who are totally or partially disabled for less than 3 days;
    *   Disabled workers;
