### Exercise: My Quest

Sit quietly for a few minutes and go deep inside yourself to ask the question, who am I and why am I here. Try to think beyond your roles as mother, father, boss, colleague etc to that person within. Then using the materials given to you, create a 'Personal Life Flag or banner' to show this to you and the world.

**What is my personal quest and higher purpose in life?**

### Business Goals

Before we get started with our business purpose and vision, let's start by drafting some business goals. Begin by jotting down your recent performance and use this to work forward and project your growth into the future.

'Other' is any measure that YOU use to identify your progress. It could be profit, new clients, new products or new territories.

> "The clearer you are about what you want the more likely you are to achieve it." – **Billy Cox**
