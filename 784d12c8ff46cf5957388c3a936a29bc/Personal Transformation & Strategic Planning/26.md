### 7.5. Social & Environmental

**Social & Environmental:** This Pillar also incorporates how you interact with the communities in which you operate, and the world at large. Attending to the welfare, wellbeing and performance of the world and having a positive social impact is clearly a Pillar of success! Increasingly, attention to the effects of your business on the environment and the 360 stakeholder or ‘triple bottom line’ is important.

---

### 7.6. Defining Your Five Pillars

Whilst every business has the same five main Pillars, each business may define them slightly differently and according to their own needs.

Some examples to explain what the Pillars can mean in a business are shown below, for the purpose of this workshop we will use these for now as we develop your plan. You can refine them further after the workshop using the template provided, if you feel it necessary.

*   **PEOPLE:** We employ, train and develop the best people for the job
*   **SYSTEMS AND PROCESSES:** We use the most efficient, up to date systems & methods to manage our business
*   **FINANCE AND GOVERNANCE:** We are a profitable business, built on efficient administration and good governance
*   **CUSTOMERS:** Our Customer needs drive the success of our business
*   **SOCIAL & ENVIRONMENTAL:** Our business strives to create positive social impact and operates in an environmentally responsible way
