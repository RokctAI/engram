## 11. Putting it All Together – The Plan on a Page

Now that you have completed all the different elements of your Plan on a Page, it is time to put it all together into one document.

To do this, you simply need to fill in the different areas of the template provided, with the content you have developed for each section. To help you, we have included a blank template, as well as one that has been populated, so that you can see exactly how the different areas come together to form a cohesive Plan.

Even though our Purpose is not on the Plan, remember it and keep it visible.
