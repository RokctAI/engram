## 6. Administration, Compliance
*   Full set of standard Business templates including Invoice, Quote, Statement, Delivery Note, Order etc – print & online
*   Backup of records (either manual, hard drive etc)
*   Legal & statutory compliance in place
*   Business Registration in place
*   Efficient Filing- Information management Systems in place
*   Governance Structures in place

## 7. HR systems
*   Staff Contracts & Job descriptions
*   Up to date Policies & Procedures (Written)
*   Employee relations: Disciplinary procedures in place
*   Employee training & development plan
*   Succession plan in place for key positions in the business

## 8. Sales
*   Effective Salesperson/s in place
*   Sales agent/team (external)
*   Full set of product samples (If you are a manufacturer) or service overview
*   Customer Relations Management (CRM) system in place to manage the sales process
*   Quarterly sales plan and targets
