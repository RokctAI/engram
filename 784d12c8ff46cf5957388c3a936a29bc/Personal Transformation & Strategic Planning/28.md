> To complete the exercise for your own business, please list the Strategic Objectives needed to make the five Pillars in your business happen. You may write as many things on the list as you feel necessary but try to be concise – some Pillars may have more than others, but you will trim these down as per below.

You will see from these examples that some Objectives could be placed under more than one heading. For example, you could put production-related objectives under Processes or Customers, and sales-related ones under Customers or Finance. This is fine – just decide which Pillar to use and stick to it.

```
Your comfort zone ───▶ (Comfort Zone)     (Where the magic happens)
                                             [Large Area]
```
