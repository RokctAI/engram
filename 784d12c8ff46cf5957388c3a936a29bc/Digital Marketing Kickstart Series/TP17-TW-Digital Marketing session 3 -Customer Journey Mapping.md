### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 13 -->
HOW TO BUILD A JOURNEY MAP
| # | Step | Description | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Define personas | Pick 1 to 3 priority segments | e.g. "First-time online buyer, 25–34, mobile-first" — focus on the segment that drives the most revenue or churn risk. |
| 2 | Outline stages | Customise the 8 standard stages | e.g. Awareness → Consideration → Purchase → Onboarding → Use → Support → Renewal → Advocacy. Adapt names to your business. |
| 3 | Map touchpoints | Every brand interaction | e.g. Google ad, website landing page, WhatsApp enquiry, in-store visit, delivery SMS, follow-up email. |
| 4 | Document actions | What customers do at each one | e.g. At "Consideration" they compare prices on 3 sites, read reviews, and ask a friend before adding to cart. |
| 5 | Capture emotions | Highs, lows, frustration, delight | e.g. Excited at unboxing (high), confused by setup instructions (low), relieved after live-chat support resolves it. |
| 6 | Find pain points | Where they drop off or stall | e.g. 60% abandon cart at shipping cost reveal; 30% never complete the account verification email step. |
| 7 | Assign ownership | Who runs each touchpoint | e.g. Marketing owns ads + landing page; Sales owns enquiry response; Ops owns delivery; CX owns post-purchase support. |
| 8 | Find opportunities | Prioritise by impact and effort | e.g. Add live shipping cost on product page (high impact, low effort) before redesigning the whole checkout flow. |

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 15 -->
1. Content strategy
AWARENESS content for early prospects, COMPARISON guides for decision stage

Customers need different content at different stages.

Awareness-stage prospects want educational, problem-framing content

![](Picture7.jpg)
Mismatched content kills conversion.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 16 -->
2. Channel Selection
Invest where customers ACTUALLY RESEARCH, not where YOU PREFER to post.

Capitec Invested heavily in physical branches in townships and rural towns when competitors retreated to digital-only.

Target customers still preferred in-person account opening.

![](Image1.jpg)

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 17 -->
3. Message timing
SEQUENCE emails/SMS/DM to match the typical consideration window

Triggers urgency emails ("Only 2 rooms left at this price") timed to the average user's research window of 3–7 days, NOT IMMEDIATELY after the first search.

![](Picture6.jpg)

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 18 -->
4. Removing Pain Points
Simplify the ONBOARDING, the checkout, the booking flow

![](Picture6.jpg)
Account opening from 30+ minutes at a branch to under 5 minutes at a Pick n Pay kiosk.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 19 -->

Welcome packs, the unboxing, first POST-SALES call experience

![](Image1.jpg)
Apple spends years ENGINEERING the UNBOXING experience.

The slow lid release, layered presentation, premium materials and the scent of the box

| --- | --- | --- |
| E-commerce | Limited-time offer, abandoned cart, simple checkout | EFT + mobile money options matter |
