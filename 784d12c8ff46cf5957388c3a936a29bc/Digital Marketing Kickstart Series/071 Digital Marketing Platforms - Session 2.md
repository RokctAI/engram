# Digital Marketing Platforms - Session 2

_Type: ebook · Section: Digital Marketing Kickstart Series_

## HubSpot Introduction

This session introduced the core principles of digital marketing and gave a practical overview of HubSpot as an all-in-one tool to manage marketing activities. Participants explored how HubSpot can support customer attraction, lead tracking and performance measurement, and how it fits into a simple, scalable digital marketing setup for small businesses.

## Getting started with HubSpot

Not every business needs every platform. Find your business type, then focus only on the platforms in your row. This keeps setup fast and avoids paying for tools you will not use.
