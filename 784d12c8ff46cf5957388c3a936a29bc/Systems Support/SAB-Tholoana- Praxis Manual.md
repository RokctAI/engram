8. Saving the page and exiting the Portal

Save your work regularly. The SAB Foundation Portal is a web page and the server will time out after 19 minutes of no activity. Each
time you save the server will refresh and give you a further 19 minutes. At any stage you can see how much time you have left but
looking at the top of the screen. See screen shot below.

Each time you save the system will check what data is still missing and inform you with a maroon coloured instruction on the top of the
page.

You can save and close the system by clicking on logout at any stage and then login again to complete the form.

11

9. Validating and Submitting

Each time you save the page by clicking the save button the system will tell you what information is still missing. This appears in maroon
on the top of the page. When all the information has been entered you will see the blue message without any maroon instructions.

You can then click the submit button on the top of your page. This will alert the SAB Foundation that your report has been submitted. You
will still be able to view your report but you will not be able to edit it.

10.  Discussion Board

You will see the discussion board on the right-hand side of your report page. If you have any questions you can put them on the
discussion board and someone form the SAB Foundation Tholoana Enterprise Team will reply to you on the same discussion board.
