FT Portal – User Manual

The FT Portal platform will be your go-to for almost everything – important programme announcements, mentor catch
up notes, the programme team comments and updates on your programme milestones.

How do you access this?

Visit: https://portal.fetola.co.za/

Log in using the username and password shared via email with you

Once logged in your landing page will consist of the following;

-  My alerts

o  Feedback notifications

-  My announcements

o

Important programme announcements

How does it work?

•  Please review your profile to ensure that your details are relevant and correctly captured, if not, please update

accordingly.

•  Monthly Reporting will be the  submission of your financials including your income  statement and balance

sheet.

•  Your mentor notes comments, action points and successes from your mentorship sessions in the  ‘Mentor:

Milestone Comments, Financial Comments and My Mentor Comments’ – This is updated regularly.

•  You  can  then  log  in,  review  these  comments  and  add  your  own  notes  in  the  ‘Entrepreneur:  Milestone

Comments, Financial Comments and My Comments’.

•  The  programme  team  can  view  all  comments;  find  opportunities,  challenges  etc  and  provide  additional

support if necessary.

What is the aim?

•  Track your recommended action points,
•  Flag business challenges you may have – we will be notified and ready to assist,
•  View your financials, track your business growth,
•  View, monitor and update your milestones
