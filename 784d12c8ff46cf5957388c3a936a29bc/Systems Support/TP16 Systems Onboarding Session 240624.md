Job Types and Definitions

Permanent Jobs

• A new permanent job has an employment contract that states that the job is fulltime and there is

no specified end date.

Permanent Part-time Jobs
• The employee has a permanent contract [no specified end date] but does not work fulltime.
• E.g they may only work on certain days, and are contracted for only a certain number of hours

per month

Permanent Seasonal Jobs

• The employee has a permanent contract with no specific end date, stating the employee only works

during specific periods, e.g. harvest, festive season, school holiday etc.

Job Types and Definitions

Fixed-term Jobs
• A new fixed-term job is one that has been created for a fixed period [at least 12 months] depends on the

needs on the business.

Short-term / Temporary Jobs
• A new job that has been created and has a contract in place, but is designed to exist only for a finite

amount of time that is less than 12 months.

Informal Jobs

• This is a job with no formal contract in place, and where the salary is below the minimum wage.
• E.g. waste pickers
