Problem: 7 out of 10 early-stage SMEs in South Africa  still struggle with accessing finance for their growth needs. Barriers to access do exist, both on the supply-side (relating mainly to the structure of the financing landscape) and on the demand-side (investment readiness of small businesses).
As Fetola’s Investment readiness team, Our role is to facilitate access to stage-appropriate financing for the businesses that we serve; and to help entrepreneurs better prepare for successful financing outcomes as supports their long-term sustainability.

Facilitate access to programme-related  investment funds

Mokoji Trading and Projects sells broiler chickens. They retails to community members either on a small scale for their private consumption or in bulk for individuals and organizations who resell these in nearby locations.

During the programme period Mokoji not only built her business confidence, she created strong community networks and increased her production capacity from two chicken houses to six. To support increased production she increased her staff by 100%, going from 3 to 6 employees. She was better able to manage her cashflow and increase her margins which she reinvested in her business. She also received a bakkie as part of her grant disbursement which has helped her to deliver her products and visit potential clients

# REPORTING ON PRAXIS

Sales* is amounts received because of running your business. In some reports, it can also be referred to as turnover.
Total income* is equal or greater than sales*.
Expenses* amounts paid out as result of running a business. Made up of cost of sales and other operational expenses
 Balance sheet

Total debts*: also referred as total liabilities on the template. Sum of short and long-term liabilities.
Total assets*: comprise of sum of short-term and long-term assets.
