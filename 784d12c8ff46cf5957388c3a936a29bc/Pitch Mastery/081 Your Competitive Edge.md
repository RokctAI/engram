# Your Competitive Edge

_Type: ebook · Section: Pitch Mastery_

The competitive advantage slide is an opportunity to demonstrate to potential investors why your business stands out from the competition by highlighting what differentiates you in the market. In this lesson, we will examine the key components and strategies for creating an effective competitive advantage slide.

## Example

Imagine you're in a town with three pizza joints. All of them make good pizza, but one offers a free topping of the customer's choice. This sets it apart and attracts more customers.

A competitive advantage is like a special spice that makes your pizza taste different and more appealing than the others. It's what makes your business unique and gives it an edge over competitors in the same market.

## Types of competitive advantage

- **Cost advantage:** "TechCom" makes fantastic phones without spending a fortune: efficient ways to obtain parts, bargains with suppliers, fast manufacturing. They can sell top-quality phones without breaking the bank.
- **Innovation advantage:** "InnoCell" has a holographic display in their phones, like a 3D movie without glasses. No one else has this technology, making them the trendsetters.
- **Differentiation advantage:** "FreshBake" creates pastries that stand out from the usual vanilla, chocolate and strawberry. They may be pricier, but people who want unique flavours turn to them.

## Sustainable advantage

Is your competitive advantage sustainable? Can it withstand market fluctuations and evolving trends?

Consider "GreenGlow Cars", whose eco-friendly technology is not a one-time novelty: it keeps evolving, and their engineers keep finding ways to make their cars greener and more efficient.
