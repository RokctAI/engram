# The Intro Slide

_Type: ebook · Section: Pitch Mastery_

## Your name and position

Introduce yourself! Let others know who you are and what your role is within the company. This isn't just about sharing facts; it's more like a virtual handshake that adds a personal touch to your pitch.

When people can connect a name to a role, it enhances the credibility of your message. A personal introduction sets the tone and demonstrates that you are genuinely committed and capable, leading with both expertise and passion.

## Background and context

Provide a clear picture of where your business is headed. Imagine you're giving directions to a friend: wouldn't you mention if you're going to the beach or the mountains? Similarly, in your pitch, it's important to acknowledge the environment your business operates in. Are you in the tech, agriculture, or fashion industry?

This context helps your audience understand the essence of your venture. It's like saying, "We're riding the wave in [Industry/Sector]," which offers a brief insight into your business and sets the stage for what's to come.

## Your product and services

Share a brief overview of what you're selling or the services you provide. Explain to your audience why you're passionate about this business and the goals that drive your efforts.
