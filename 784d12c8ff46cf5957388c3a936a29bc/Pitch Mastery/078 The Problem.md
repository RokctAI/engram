# The Problem

_Type: ebook · Section: Pitch Mastery_

The problem slide in your pitch deck should highlight the issues, challenges, or gaps in the market that your business aims to address. This slide is crucial to your pitch because a clear understanding of the problem sets the stage for presenting your solution. In this lesson, we will guide you through the process of creating an impactful problem slide.

## Outline the pain points

Explain why this problem is important and who is affected. Think of it as storytelling: create a vivid picture of why this problem is the 'villain' in your business narrative. Who is struggling because of it? Why does it need a hero (your solution)?

You're not just listing facts. You're making it personal, like sharing a relatable story with a friend. Imagine you're in a coffee shop, chatting about a common annoyance that everyone understands. That's the key: connecting your audience to the problem so they nod along, saying, 'Yeah, I've been there too.'

## Quantify the impact

Zoom out and see the big picture. Show how this issue isn't just an annoyance; it's causing ripples in the pond of life.

Imagine you're the detective and you've got the data as your sidekick. Grab those numbers and stats to back you up. How many people are affected? What's the financial hit? How's it messing with the flow of things? Throw in some graphs or charts. Prove that this problem isn't just a minor hiccup; it's a hurricane, and your solution is the calm after the storm.

## Create a bridge

Give them a sneak peek into the solution. This is like a preview before the main event, where we share just enough information to spark their interest.

Imagine you're cooking something delicious. You reveal a hint of the secret ingredient that makes your recipe stand out, but you keep the full recipe a mystery. In the same way, provide a glimpse of how your business swoops in like a hero to address those challenges, leaving them eager to learn more.
