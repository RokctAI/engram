# Pitch Submission Instructions

_Type: ebook · Section: Pitch Mastery_

The Investment Readiness team is here to help you understand your investment potential and guide you on how to package your investment case.

## 1. Video pitch submission

As part of the pitch evaluation process, you submit a short video pitch on your business. It may be recorded with a cell phone, camcorder or similar device. Accepted formats: 3gp, mp4, avi, mkv, avchd, wmv and mov.

The video pitch should be 2 minutes, covering:

- A short introduction about yourself and your business
- The problem you are solving
- The proposed solution
- Business model
- Competitive advantage: what makes you different
- Your ask

The team reviews video pitches as they come in, to allow corrections and enhancements so submissions meet the requirements.

## 2. Pitch deck submission

Your second submission is your pitch deck. It is a reference tool for your video submission and details your request for funding. Use the Pitch Deck Template as a guide to the points it should include.

You are encouraged to add enhancements (videos, pictures, testimonials, etc.). The onus is on you to make sure it shows everything you want the investment committee to see.

Upload the deck in PowerPoint (pptx/ppt) format.
