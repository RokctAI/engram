## 2. Investment Readiness Portfolio

The Investment Readiness team facilitates access to financing for businesses and helps better prepare them for successful financing outcomes.

Key programme interventions:

- **Pitch Training:** helps entrepreneurs enhance their pitching skills to attract investors and other key stakeholders. It is also the kick-off point for pitch submissions.
- **Grant Funding:** a micro investment of R3,500 as a start-up grant to support business formalisation and target market identification. Follow-on seed financing of R25,000 is granted on the back of successful phase 1 investments, for product development, sales and marketing.
