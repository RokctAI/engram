# Your Ask

_Type: ebook · Section: Pitch Mastery_

The "Ask Slide" in your pitch deck outlines your funding requirements and what you expect from potential investors. This lesson helps you create a clear and compelling Ask Slide that attracts investors and stakeholders to support your venture.

## Let's define it

Imagine you run a lemonade stand and your goal is to sell 50 cups to buy a new board game. The Ask Slide is the moment you clearly tell others, "I need to sell 50 cups to get that game."

## Example

Why is this important? If you never told your friends how many cups you needed to sell or what the money was for, they might not buy any lemonade, or only a few cups. The Ask Slide is being clear about how many cups you need to sell and how you plan to use the money.

## The magic of clarity

Being clear about how much funding you need and what you'll use it for is like setting a target for your lemonade sales. It helps investors understand your plan, making them more likely to support you in reaching your goal.
