# Your Track Record

_Type: ebook · Section: Pitch Mastery_

The track record slide in your pitch deck highlights your past achievements and milestones, which reinforces your credibility and competence. In this lesson, we will explore how to create an impactful track record slide.

## Key components of an achievement slide

- **Past successes and milestones:** key achievements, projects, or milestones that show your venture's journey so far.
- **Financial growth:** data or graphs of revenue growth, profit margins, and other financial achievements over time.
- **Clientele and partnerships:** notable clients, partnerships, or collaborations that show credibility and market reach.
- **Awards and recognition:** any awards, accolades, or recognitions your venture has received.

## Tell a story

- **Narrative approach:** craft a story about your journey, the challenges you've overcome and the key turning points.
- **Emphasise learning and growth:** show how past experiences have built the current strengths of your venture.
- **Tell your tale:** your venture is a hero on an adventure. Early on you faced the 'Dragons of Doubt', when everyone questioned whether your idea would succeed, and you fought through the scepticism.
- **Turning the tides:** in the 'Valley of Challenges', just when it seemed you might stumble, you found the 'Bridge of Innovation' that carried you forward.
- **Learning at every turn:** the 'Forest of Feedback' taught you to listen and improve; the 'Cave of Collaboration' showed the power of partnerships.
- **The hero's transformation:** each battle and triumph has made you a wiser hero, ready for bigger quests.

Every venture has its own adventure. Your track record is the story of how you became the hero of your venture, battle scars and all.
