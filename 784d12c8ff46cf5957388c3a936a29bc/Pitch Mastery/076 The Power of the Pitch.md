# The Power of the Pitch

_Type: ebook · Section: Pitch Mastery_

A pitch is a brief and persuasive presentation of your idea, business, product, or service. It's your opportunity to capture attention and demonstrate the unique value you offer. If you deliver it effectively, investors may become interested in your project. Additionally, a great pitch can engage other key stakeholders, including customers, partners, and your own team.

In a competitive landscape where everyone is trying to be noticed, a well-crafted pitch helps your idea shine and stand out from the crowd.

## How long should it be?

Starting with elevator pitches is an excellent approach when you're learning how to pitch effectively. An elevator pitch is a brief, persuasive conversation that lasts around 30 to 60 seconds, designed to grab attention and ensure that your audience remembers you.

For our course, however, we want to take it a step further. We ask that you upload a 2-minute video pitch where you share detailed information about your business. Please include the following: what your business is all about, the problem you're addressing, your innovative solution, how your business generates revenue, what sets you apart from the competition, and the amount of funding you are seeking.

## Pitch Elements

- Introduction
- Problem
- Solution
- Business Model
- Competitors
- Your Request
