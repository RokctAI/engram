# The Business Model

_Type: ebook · Section: Pitch Mastery_

The business model slide in your pitch deck serves as a blueprint for how your venture plans to generate revenue and sustain itself in the market. Investors closely scrutinise it to assess the viability and potential profitability of your business. In this lesson, we will explore the key components and strategies for creating an impactful business model slide.

## Revenue streams

Revenue streams are the various sources through which cash flows into your business: sales, subscriptions, licensing fees, or any other methods that generate income.

It's also important to outline the key activities and resources required to create and deliver your product or service to customers.

## Explain your customer segments

Customer segments are the individuals or groups you want to impress, like curating the guest list for your ultimate event: the groups and communities you want to invite to your product or service.

Imagine you have several circles of friends: some are into gaming, some love fitness, and others are avid readers. Each friend group is a distinct customer segment with unique tastes and preferences. Your job is to have offerings that resonate with your chosen audience.

## Highlight your value proposition

Consider your business as a stage, with the value proposition as the spotlight that emphasises what makes your offer unique.

Think of a friend who intuitively understands you and always knows just what to say. Your value proposition is similar: it identifies your customers' problems and confidently reassures them, "I have the solution for you!"
