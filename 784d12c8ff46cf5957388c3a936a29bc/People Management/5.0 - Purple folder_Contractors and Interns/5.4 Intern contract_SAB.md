**This letter confirms your acceptance as a graduate Intern at Fetola for a fixed term period starting 17 February 2014 until 16 May 2014. (3 months). Contract will be up for extension to 6 months by mutual agreement.**

**What is a contract/ agreement?**

A contract or work agreement is a written document outlining the responsibilities of the Intern, and the company. It includes general guidelines, some information on the labour law and confirmation of you role and remuneration. As with any legal document you should read through it thoroughly before signing.

**What is Internship:**

The definition of an intern is “*a student or a recent graduate undergoing supervised practical training”.* This is therefore a learning opportunity in the workplace environment and should not be confused with employment.

**What is a Stipend:**

A stipend is “*a predetermined amount of money that is provided to help offset expenses. Stipends are often provided (to those who are ineligible to receive a salary) in exchange for the duties they perform, such as interns. A stipend is generally lower than a salary would be, because the recipient is at the same time able to gain experience and knowledge in a specific field.”*

**Objective of Internship:**

The objective of the Fetola internship is to provide recent graduates an opportunity to apply what they have learned from their coursework in a “real life” environment. The internship encourages the student to think critically and to constantly engage with the work environment in order to expand their skills in a practical, work-related manner. The internship also is expected to heighten the individual’s awareness of community issues, motivate learners to create opportunities, embrace new ideas, and give direction to positive change.

The Fetola internship is a real work experience and will require the intern to deliver results to a high standard in practical, value-adding tasks. Success on these practical tasks will form a key part of performance reviews.

The intention is that graduate interns leave Fetola inspired and better equipped to be successful members of society – as employees or entrepreneurs.

**Scope of Responsibilities:**

As an Intern, you have agreed to work diligently on a range of practical applied tasks that will be asked of you. A high level of personal responsibility, careful attention to goals and specific deadlines, and a striving to achieve high quality results is expected. The specific details of your scope of work will be advised.

You will be required to work in the Fetola team as a functioning, value-adding participant, under supervision and reporting to a senior staff member. Training support and mentorship will be provided during the course of the normal working day.

**Deadlines:**

We consider all **specific deadlines** to be of great importance. Task deadlines should be taken seriously and full completion of your responsibilities is expected.

**Remuneration – Internship Stipend :**

You will be paid a monthly intern stipend of R3000.00 for the days completed**.** You will be expected to complete tasks allocated to you and partially completed and poor quality work will be viewed in a negative light.

You will not be entitled to additional stipend for any work in excess of the task, or time unless otherwise negotiated with the programme director. Should you not attend work you will not be paid the stipend.

Payment shall be made on a monthly basis to your bank account with the monthly salary run.

Fetola shall deduct any payment required by the Law from your stipend.

**Hours of Work:**

A minimum of 40 hours of actual attendance at work per week is expected and normal work hours are from 08h00 to 16h30 Monday to Friday. Additional work is encouraged where deadlines are pressing, but Fetola does not pay overtime rates.

**Disbursements:**

Interns will be refunded for any expenditure related to Fetola, including mileage used in the performance of duties but excluding travel to and from the office or place of work.

**Breach & Termination:**

you fail to comply with the terms and conditions of this Agreement, or fail in your performance as an intern, Fetola may terminate your contract immediately and will be further entitled to withhold payment of your remuneration in respect of tasks not completed by you.

In addition, as it is our responsibility to ensure that your learning opportunity is a positive, mutual beneficial one, we reserve the right to terminate this agreement where there is a mismatch of capabilities, or needs.

**General**

You are expected to ensure at all times that your professional and associated personal conduct whilst a Fetola Intern enhances the professional reputation and standing of the company, the client (eg Old Mutual Foundation), beneficiaries and partners.

Fetola prides itself with the quality and calibre of its team members and standards of conduct and professionalism are of extreme importance both in and outside the office.

**Behaviour & Dress Code**

It is the policy of the company that team members should project a casual but professional image. You should exercise good taste and common sense at all times. Casual, clean and neat attire is the appropriate dress for Fetola representatives.

The following are non-exclusive list of dress that is <span class="underline">unacceptable</span>:

  - Clothing that is worn, dirty or frayed

  - Bare feet

  - Clothing that is too revealing

  - Extreme short skirts

  - Sheer or see-through clothing

  - Clothing with potentially offensive words, graphics or logos

  - Sweatpants or exercise pants

 The standard of communications, use of language and behaviour of all employees should fit the professional standards of Fetola. Communications should be at all times use appropriate business language & all communication on Fetola Letterhead or from official Fetola emails must be approved by senior management before being sent out.

It is imperative that you understand that we share our space with tenants and it is of utmost importance that a professional environment is maintained in the office at all times.

**Confidentiality**:

You will keep confidential all the information acquired by you during the course of your contract with Fetola.

You agree that for the duration of this agreement with Fetola, and for 2 years thereafter, you shall not for the purposes of your own direct or indirect gain, utilise, sell, distribute or in any other way use any information that you gain access to by virtue of your appointment with Fetola and/or the knowledge you acquire about the relevant beneficiary enterprise(s).

You further acknowledge that the provisions under this Confidentiality heading are reasonable and necessary in light of the access you will be given to the confidential business information during the course of working here. It is agreed that each provision under this Confidentiality heading is independent and severable from the other provisions.
