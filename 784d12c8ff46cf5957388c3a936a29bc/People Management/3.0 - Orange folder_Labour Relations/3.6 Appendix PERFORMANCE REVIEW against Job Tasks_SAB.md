**KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS**

**Job Summary**

As a Sales business manager, you have agreed to work diligently on a range of practical applied tasks that will be asked of you. A high level of personal responsibility, careful attention to goals and specific target, and a striving to achieve high quality results is expected. The specific details of your scope of work will be advised.

You will be required to work in the Cold Press Media team as a functioning, value-adding participant, under supervision and reporting to a senior staff member. Training support and mentorship will be provided during the course of the normal working day.

 

**General**

You are expected to ensure at all times that your professional and associated personal conduct whilst a Sales Business Manager enhances the professional reputation and standing of the company, the publications, beneficiaries and partners.

Cold Press Media prides itself with the quality and calibre of its team members and standards of conduct and professionalism are of extreme importance both in and outside the office.

The standard of communications, use of language and behaviour of all employees should fit the professional standards of Cold Press Media. Communications should be at all times use appropriate business language.

The ability to listen to, understand and follow instructions, and the willingness to be proactive and self-reliant in the solving of problems and completion of tasks is expected, meeting of targets, Inherent in the Cold Press media culture is continual improvement and innovation and you are encouraged to contribute ideas that could improve our collective performance.
