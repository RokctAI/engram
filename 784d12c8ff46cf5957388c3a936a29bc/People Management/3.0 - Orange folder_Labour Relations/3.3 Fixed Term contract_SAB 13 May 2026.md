3.  **SALARY/WAGE**
    
    1.  You shall receive a basic salary/wage of R……………….. per ……………, payable in arrears, from which deduction for PAYE, unemployment insurance, pension and medical aid fund contributions, where applicable, shall be made. In addition, deduction shall be made of any other amount authorised by you and agreed to by the Company.

    3.  You hereby agree that the company may deduct from your salary at any time during your employment or on its termination, any sums which you may owe to the Company, including monies for any actual damage or loss which you have been found to be liable for during and enquiry. Such deductions will not exceed 25% of the remuneration owing to you at any time.

4.  **HOURS OF WORK**
    
    1.  > You shall be obliged to work 45 (forty five) normal hours weekly (exclusive of meal breaks). These normal hours comprise of 8 normal hours per day if you work a 6 day week, and 9 hours per day if you work a 5 day week. Your Manager shall communicate your daily working hours to you.
    
    2.  > You will be entitled to a meal break of 1 hour / 30 minutes *(delete which is not applicable)* after no more than 5 (five) consecutive hours worked by you.
    
    3.  > You hereby agree to work overtime within the statutorily prescribed maxima, currently 10 (ten) hours per week, provided that:

1)  > The Company gives you notice during the working day prior to the day on which you are required to work overtime;

2)  > Nothing herein shall limit the Company’s right and your obligation to work emergency overtime on short notice;

3)  > Should you work more than your normal hours as outlined above, this will be considered overtime and you will be paid at a rate of time and a half of your normal hourly rate. Alternatively, you may be given time off in lieu of such payment, which time will be granted to you within 12 (twelve) months of you becoming entitled to it.

5.  **NIGHT WORK**
    
    1.  Any work which you may perform between 18h00 and 06h00 the following day will be considered to be ‘night work’.
    
    2.  Should you do night work, you will be entitled to a night shift allowance of an additional ……% of your normal hourly wage / R……. for each night shift worked. (Delete whichever is not applicable)

6.  **SUNDAY WORK**
    
    1.  Should you be required to work on a Sunday, you shall be paid for any time worked at a rate of twice your normal hourly payment rate. However, should Sunday be or become a normal working day due to the nature of the company’s business, then you will be paid at a rate of time and a half of your normal hourly rate.
    
    2.  You hereby agree that you may be given time off in lieu of the additional Sunday pay, which will be granted to you within 12 (twelve) months of becoming entitled to it.

7.  **PUBLIC HOLIDAYS**
    
    1.  You will be entitled to paid leave on all statutory public holidays as per the Paid Public Holidays Act and/or amendments thereto.

2.  Should you be required to work on a public holiday, you shall be paid for any time worked at a rate of twice your normal daily payment rate. Alternatively, in accordance with Section 2(2) of the Public Holidays Act, you herby agree to exchange a public holiday for another paid day off, which will be agreed upon by the parties. This exchange for another day is in lieu of the additional pay that you would otherwise be entitled to.

8.  **ANNUAL LEAVE**
    
    1.  You will accumulate leave at a rate of 1 (one) day for every 17 (seventeen) days worked by you and any leave not taken at the time of termination of this contract shall be paid out to you in lieu of such leave.
    
    2.  Any annual leave that is taken in terms of this section must first be authorised in writing by the company.
    
    3.  Your leave pay will be paid to you on your normal pay day.

9.  **PARENTAL LEAVE**
    
    1.  Parents are entitled to 4 months and 10 days of parental leave shared between the parents.
    
    2.  The birth mother should take at least 4 weeks prior to the birth of the baby and at least 6 weeks after the birth, should the leave allocation be split between the parents.
    
    3.  It is your responsibility to notify the company in writing of the date on which you intend to commence parental leave and the date on which you intend to return to work. Such notification must be given at least 4 weeks prior to the commencement of your parental leave for the birth mother. In the case of adoptions or surrogacy, at least one month’s written notice is required.
    
    4.  Parents should be employed for at least four months to qualify for parental leave.
    
    5.  Parental leave is unpaid and payment can be claimed through the Unemployment Insurance Fund.

10. **FAMILY RESPONSIBILITY LEAVE**
    
    1.  Once you have been in the employ of the company for more than 4 months, you will be entitled to 3 days paid leave per annum on the occurrence of any of the following events:

<!-- end list -->

1)  The birth of your child (if you are a male employee);

2)  The illness of a child of yours who is under the age of 18 (eighteen);

3)  The death of your spouse, life partner, parent, adoptive parent, grandparent, child, adopted child, grandchild or sibling.
    
    1.  Before receiving payment, the company may require you to produce reasonable proof of the event for which you are claiming family responsibility leave.

<!-- end list -->

11. **SICK LEAVE**
    
    1.  You shall accumulate paid sick leave at a rate of one day in respect of every 26 (twenty six) days worked by you.
    
    2.  You will be paid your normal wage during your period of sick leave, provided that should you be absent from work:

<!-- end list -->

  - for more than 2 (two) consecutive work days; or

  - on more than 2 (two) occasions within a period of 8 (eight) weeks,

> You will be required to produce a certificate signed by a registered medical practitioner stating the duration and nature of your incapacity. Should you fail to provide such a certificate, the Company may withhold payment for the days on which you were absent and may take disciplinary action for unauthorised absenteeism. Should the company so require, you agree to see a medical practitioner of the company’s choosing, for a second opinion.

12. **NOTIFICATION OF ABSENCE**

> Should you, for any reason, not be able to attend work, you are required to let your Manager know by no later than your normal starting time. Should you fail without good cause to contact the Company within this period, the Company may treat your failure to notify the Company as a disciplinary offence.

13. **EXEMPTIONS FROM THE REGULATION OF WORKING TIME**

> Should you be a senior manager or your gross salary be in excess of the amount stipulated in Basic Conditions of Employment Act, updated on 1 May 2026, currently R 269 600.90 or any future amendments thereto, you shall not be entitled to either overtime payment or additional pay for working night shift or on Sundays or Public Holidays, neither will you be entitled to time off in lieu of such payment.

14. **PROOF OF CITIZENSHIP**

> Upon commencement of your employment, you will be required to provide proof of your South African citizenship. Alternatively, should you not be a South African citizen, you will be required to provide either a work permit or proof of permanent residency. Please note that your failure to produce either of the above will render the employment agreement null and void (i.e. of no force or effect) due to the fact that the company is then not in a position to legally employ you.

15. **MEDICAL EXAMINATION**

> The company shall be entitled at any time during your employment to require you, where justified or required by legislation, to undergo a medical examination by a medical practitioner of its choice for the purposes of an enquiry into your health / welfare and/or into your capacity to continue in employment with the Company. The outcome of such examination/s shall however not be utilised to unfairly discriminate against you.

16. **SEARCHES**

> You may be required to submit yourself to searches of your person and possessions by a person designated by the Company. Failure to abide by this will be seen as a serious disciplinary offence, which may lead to your dismissal.

17. **COMPANY RULES**

> You agree to abide by all the Company's procedures, work rules, disciplinary rules and health/safety rules, as amended from time to time. A copy of these rules is available from the company for your perusal.

18. **CHANGES TO WAGES AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT**

> Your terms and conditions of employment are further subject to, and may be varied from time to time, after consultation with you, by a Company review or legislation changes.

19. **CONFIDENTIALITY**

> You shall not at any time divulge to any person any trade secrets or any other confidential information concerning the Company's business or affairs nor that of any of its associates without the Company's written consent. Failure to abide by this clause will be seen as a serious disciplinary offence, which may lead to your dismissal.

20. **USE OF COMPANY’S COMPUTERS**

> You hereby agree that the company has the right to access, monitor, read, filter, block, delete, use and act upon any incoming or outgoing e-mail message sent or received by the you, attachments to such e-mails, hyperlinks in such e-mail messages or attachments, Web sites visited by you and files or records saved automatically or by you on the company's equipment.

21. **POLYGRAPH AND BREATHALYSER TESTS**

> In order to preserve the relationship of trust that exists between the Company and its employees, the Company reserves the right to require you to undertake a polygraph or breathalyzer test at any point in time. Should you refuse to undergo either test, such refusal will be seen in a serious light as it amounts to a refusal to carry out a reasonable instruction and it may therefore result in your dismissal.

22. **GENERAL**
    
    1.  You will be required to undertake responsibility for all apparatus, equipment and materials provided to you for the carrying out of your duties. All material (physical and intellectual) acquired by yourself in the course of your employment will remain the property of the company. In addition, you are required to adhere to all operating and compliance policies that may be in force from time to time.
    
    2.  You are expected to perform your duties in the best interests of the company and refrain from any action which may in any manner harm the good name and reputation of the company or which may place the company in an invidious or compromising position. As such you are expected to display total honesty and integrity in the performance of your duties and to exercise appropriate care and skill.
    
    3.  Please note that no private work, of any nature, may be undertaken by you. You are not permitted to work either for yourself or for any other employer, or offer your services in a consulting capacity without the express authorisation from the company.

23. **TERMINATION**
    
    1.  Your contract of employment with the Company may be terminated by you or the Company, subject to Company procedures, upon 1 (one) weeks notice during the first 6 (six) months, 2 (two) weeks notice during the first year and on 4 (four) weeks notice after 1 (one) continuous year of service.
    
    2.  Notwithstanding the above clause your employment shall automatically terminate on the date referred to in paragraph 2 of this contract. This clause serves as notice to such effect.
    
    3.  Notice by either party shall only be given in writing.
    
    4.  Notice by either party may be given on any working day. Such notice shall be calculated from the normal working day after such notice has been given.
    
    5.  This contract may, subject to the Company's procedures, be terminated for misconduct, incapacity or the operational requirements of the Company. When there are no grounds for summary termination of the contract, notice of termination shall be given in terms of this clause.
