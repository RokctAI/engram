<p>Please sign the enclosed copy of this letter and return it to me by (specify date) to indicate your acceptance of this offer.</p>
<p>We are confident you will be able to make a significant contribution to the success of our (Company/Division/Department) and look forward to working with you.</p>

<p>______________________________________</p>
