## 4.4. GUIDELINES TO DEVELOP A JOB DESCRIPTION 

1.  A job title and the job broad purpose or mission (equivalent to elevator speech). This should be the first point of contact to greet all visitors, whether the contacts are made on the internet, via phone calls, or visitors physically come in.

2.  Job location, include the office address where the person will be placed or indicate if they will be working across different locations (for example provinces or countries).

3.  The name or designation of the person to whom the employee reports.

4.  Nature of appointment – full time, part time or fixed contract.

5.  Appointment start date – when the position will be activated.

6.  The job duties and responsibilities, scope of work, and working conditions. For example if your list is more than 10 bullets, you should probably regroup some tasks under a more meaningful umbrella.

7.  Core competencies - The qualifications and training, knowledge or experience, skills and abilities necessary to do the job. For example have a driver\`s license; master Microsoft Office software; experience in managing projects.

8.  The personal characteristics and attitudes necessary to performing that particular job to the best. For example, the candidate gives examples of being flexible; candidate demonstrates good communication skills
