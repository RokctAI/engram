**JOB SUMMARY**

You will work in a small team whose role is to coordinate the IT requirements (software and hardware) for the company, and to ensure optimal uptime. In addition to day to day maintenance of the company’s current technology infrastructure you will play an important role in identifying, creating and implementing new solutions and systems. Maintenance of the company database and communications platforms is also required

**GENERAL**

Meticulous, professional work standards are required. Reliability and the commitment to meeting deadlines is essential.
