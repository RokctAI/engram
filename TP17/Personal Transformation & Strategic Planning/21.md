### 6.2. Developing your Business Vision Statement

Developing a powerful vision statement is a crucial step in strategic planning. Creating a good vision statement isn’t that difficult. You have already thought about your WHY and jotted down the business quest or higher purpose.

Now consider the services and products you provide, then imagine you provided the very best version of them possible, over and over, to all who need them – **what would the outcome be?**

### Developing a Business Vision Statement

Vision setting is the process of defining the destination that you want to reach. As the purpose of a vision statement is to inspire, to motivate and guide a team over a long period, a good vision statement has the following characteristics:

*   It is short & easy to remember (no more than 25 words)
*   It is inspiring (it makes you and your team feel excited and enthusiastic)
*   It is realistic & real, rather than wishful and idealistic
*   Is timeless and can last for the lifetime of the business
*   It speaks to the higher purpose, the ‘why’ of the business
