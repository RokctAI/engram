## 14. Success Wheel

Business success requires mastery in all nine of these areas. You will see that they align to the Four Pillars that form an integral part of our Plan on a Page, we will explore this in more detail later on.

Let’s have a closer look at each element to make sure that we understand them all and why they are important:

| Success Wheel Element | What it Means | Why it is Important |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Legislation & Compliance** | Does your business comply with all the necessary regulations & laws of the country as well as the sector in which you operate? | Failure to comply can place you the owner, as well as the business in jeopardy. Compliance is also important in terms of being considered for tenders and larger client opportunities. |
| **2. Human Resource** | Is all your paperwork regarding your staff up to date? Do you take steps to upskill your staff, and what do you do to build a happy, productive team culture? | Compliance with HR requirements is not only important from a legal perspective, it will also lead to a happier and more productive workforce and can also protect both parties in the event of a labour dispute. In addition, a well-trained workforce will be more efficient & motivated. |
| **3. Leadership & Vision** | How well do you steer the ship? Are you open to new ideas and ways of doing things, or are you stuck in a rut? How about your team? Do they respond well to change or resist it? Is there a culture of innovation in your organization? | Strong and effective leadership is crucial to the success of any organisation. The ‘driver’ of the organisation must not only ensure he/she has the best people and systems in place, but that the staff and especially management team under his/her authority is empowered and motivated to perform. Success also requires that leadership, management and staff are comfortable with change. |
| **4. Technology & Communications** | How savvy are you and your team with technology and the use thereof to increase performance, productivity and efficiency in your business? Do you have the equipment you need to do the job properly, especially in terms of IT and communications? | Smart use of up-to-date technology can make the difference between a business that thrives and one that stagnates. With most business communication, research and interaction taking place online these days, you simply have to have access to the tools necessary to allow your team to do their jobs efficiently and professionally. |
