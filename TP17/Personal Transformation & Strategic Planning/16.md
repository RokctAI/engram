## 5. Principle #5 - Mastering the Magic of Money!

Money is on everyone’s mind but is not everyone’s favourite topic. While some people hold a positive attitude towards money, many others have limiting, often unconscious beliefs around money that hold them back. These beliefs are formed and shaped during our formative years as we observe how our parents and caregivers work with and feel about money. Sometimes our feelings towards money are so strong that we start to believe that money is the cause of, or solution to all of our problems. That’s the power of money.

### Rationale

As entrepreneurs your dream is to create a successful, profitable business. Yet so often this dream becomes stuck and the success we seek, remains just out of reach. The Money Magic roadmap helps you to recognize how your personal relationship to money – how you feel about it and how it makes you feel – influences this success. Using this powerful realisation you can step more confidently into the dos, don’ts and how to’s of financial management. Getting to grips with the importance of record-keeping and managing the money inside the business. In step three we help you to put this all together to access the finance you need to grow the business.

Our goal is that by the time you complete the Tholoana Enterprise Programme you have the confidence, skills and documents you need to access level 1 – scored credit (e.g. to buy a car on credit). To do this you will need to make a commitment to change your attitude towards money and become comfortable with your finances. By addressing these matters and becoming fully confident to master your money you will ensure your success, not just for today, but for a lifetime.
