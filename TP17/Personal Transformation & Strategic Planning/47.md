## 15. Bringing Sustainable Practices into Your Business

There’s an assumption that growing the economy will lead to improving people’s lives.

On a national scale, the wellbeing of citizens is considered to be tied to how much a country makes in a year (i.e. Gross Domestic Products - GDP – growth). This can be deceptive, since increases in GDP often come from a minority of high-income earners, and the majority who earn very little are hardly ever counted.

At Fetola, we know that not having access to capital is only one part of a more complex network of issues that people in South Africa face, including environmental changes, depleting resources and social conflict. As entrepreneurs, these issues can have very real impacts on business.

### Guidelines for Going Green

We present simple guidelines for more sensitive businesses practices. Some of these will make sense from a cost-saving perspective (water saving and energy saving) and many of these are simply good practices.

Some of these might work for you from a cost-saving perspective or could even present an opportunity to market your business differently.

### Why go green? What’s in it for me?

Will going green help you to sleep better at night? Perhaps – but not if it’s costing you more than you can afford to spend. Read on for some comforting insights into the benefits for you and your business:

*   **Direct cost-savings:** Waste reduction and water conservation, as well as reduced energy and resources, all result in financial savings.
*   **Employee productivity:** There is a connection between meaningful work and productivity.
*   **Staff health:** environmentally friendly products (detergents, chemicals, paints, etc.) are better for staff health, compared with hazardous chemicals and toxic substances. They also make the office environment more pleasant (fewer odours, less irritation for eyes, nose, and throat), which has a positive impact on productivity.
*   **Social benefits:** there is a strong link between environmental degradation and social problems. By ensuring that your office does not harm the environment, or working to actively green or enhance the environment, you contribute to a healthier society.
*   **You could well end up attracting new customers** – there are people who care enough about these issues to make purchasing decisions around the issue of environmental responsibility (e.g., choosing an item with less packaging).

### Your business: Practical Eco-tips

#### Paper

Paper eventually biodegrades in landfill sites (it is often seen as a better packaging option for this reason). But the production of paper is resource-intensive, requiring water, forest resources and energy – not to mention the air and waste emissions associated with paper production.

*   **Recycle your paper:** Set used paper aside, shred it and arrange for collection or make a monthly trip to a recycling depot.
