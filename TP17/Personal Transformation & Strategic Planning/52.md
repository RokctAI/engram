*   Anyone receiving military training;
*   Independent Contractors
*   Members of the SANDF or SAPS
*   Any worker guilty of wilful misconduct, unless they are seriously disabled or killed;
*   Anyone employed outside the RSA for 12 or more continuous months; and
*   Workers who do the majority of their work outside the RSA but which are temporarily employed in the RSA.

### BEE Compliance

#### Exempt Micro Enterprise (EME) - Affidavit:

*   Companies with a turnover of less than R10 million annually qualify as an Exempt Micro Enterprise (EME) and are automatically assigned a good BEE score (minimum BEE level 4).
*   For a business with a turnover of less than R10 million, a B-BBEE certificate is not required. An Affidavit can be completed and signed by a Commissioner of Oaths, instead of the B-BBEE certificate. Once the Affidavit has been stamped by a Commissioner of Oaths, the Affidavit serves as a BEE certificate as no other verification is required for an Exempted Micro Enterprise.
*   The affidavit is valid for 12 months.
*   An EME is allowed to be measured in terms of the Qualifying Small Enterprise scorecard if it wishes to maximise its points and move to a higher B-BBEE score.

> **Visit the CIPC website to download your affidavit.**

#### Qualifying Small Enterprise (QSE)

*   A company has annual revenue of between R10 million and R50 million qualifies as a Qualifying Small Enterprise.
*   Qualifying Small Enterprises which 100% black ownership are deemed to be B-BBEE Level 1, with 135% procurement recognition. A sworn affidavit can be completed.
*   QSEs with over 51% black ownership qualify for B-BBEE Level 2, with a 125% procurement rating. A sworn affidavit can be completed.
*   QSEs with black ownership less than 51% need to be measured according to their industry codes. Thus, a SANAS (South African National Accredited System) accredited B-BBEE verification needs to be done.

### VAT Registration

*   An entity with a turnover of more than R1 million in any 12-month consecutive period is liable for compulsory VAT registration.
*   An entity with a turnover greater than R50 000 but less than R1 million can register voluntarily as a VAT vendor.
*   Entities must complete and submit VAT101e forms to register; these are available from the SARS website or from any SARS branch.
*   VAT must be charged at 15% for all services and/or products supplied by the vendor.
*   Input VAT, VAT that can be claimed back from SARS, can only be claimed on goods to be used in the course of making supplies/supplying a service. I.e. not for items that are wholly consumed during personal use.
