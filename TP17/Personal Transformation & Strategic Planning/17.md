The Money Magic Journey begins with understanding your own intimate relationship to money and how this affects the way you manage your personal finances.

The **Financial Roadmap** helps you to set a clear framework for your Money Magic transformation. Beginning with an easy look at your goals for the next 2 years. Do you plan to purchase a new fridge, a car, or a property? Whatever it is, we will work together to develop a Financial Roadmap to get you from where you are now to where you need to be to achieve these goals.

Next, we invite you to attend two very empowering webinars that address the emotions of money and *You and your Money*. These two webinars will help you face your financial fears by clarifying your money beliefs and empower you with game-changing strategies to grow your personal wealth. Group personal finance coaching is also available for additional practical guidance.

This new knowledge of your own personal relationship with money will empower you in conversations with your Mentor who will provide guidance to further develop your business financial roadmap and schedule a site visit with a qualified accountant to review your financial systems and process and empower you with some key action points to get you going.

As a business owner, it goes without saying that you need to know and understand what is happening in your business finances in order to make wise, well-informed decisions. Knowing if you can afford to hire another staff member, invest in new technology or have enough capital to purchase extra stock for your peak trading period takes the guesswork, and stress out of your life!

An effective **Financial Management System** makes this process so much easier and more efficient. In this phase we take you through the concepts of accounting and financial management, the pros and cons of hiring a bookkeeper and ways to analyse and understand your financial information.

This journey can be tailored to your own needs – so if you are already a chartered accountant, or someone struggling with the basics, there is something for you both.
