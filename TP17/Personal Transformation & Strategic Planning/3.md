# Introduction - Vision Setting for Success

A successful journey starts with an understanding of where you are going, and why you are going there. Even if you do not have a set itinerary or are keen to keep things flexible, having a clear idea of where you are headed is crucial to your chances of getting there. Without direction, you can get lost.

Growing a business is the same. Unless you have an idea of what you are trying to achieve through your business and all the work you put in, your chances of creating that desired future are very slim. This is why having a clear vision - and a plan in place to achieve it - is so important to your long-term success.

A vision will help you steer in a focused direction and be the captain of your own boat, and a plan will help you stay on track and ultimately reach your destination!

> "If you don't know where you're going, any road will get you there." – **Lewis Carroll**

## 1. Principle #1 – Know yourself, know your business

This personal transformation and strategic planning workshop is carefully constructed to take you through two essential parts of your growth journey.

The first part is your own **personal transformation & growth**. We will help you unlock a deeper understanding of who you are and what you are on earth to achieve, by guiding you through a process of personal reflection and increased self-awareness. This is an important step towards building self-mastery, which is an essential business leadership skill.

As you work through this personal transformation process, we will help you to strengthen the link between your 'self' and your business. This will further build inspiration and strengthen your commitment to lasting success.

Lastly, we will help you to build a **powerful, actionable strategic growth plan** for your business that has all the elements you need to realize your dreams!

Many small businesses start from a simple idea and get going without too much thought about the future. Entrepreneurs are known for their ability to just jump in and get started, and we tend to worry about the details later.

> "If you fail to plan, you are planning to fail!" – **Benjamin Franklin**

Even those organisations that do begin with a solid plan can find themselves without direction some years later. Perhaps the original leader or champion has left, the environment has changed, or things have just become a bit fuzzy over time.
