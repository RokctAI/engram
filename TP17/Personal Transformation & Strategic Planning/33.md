### The Fetola EPIC Leaders model

```
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
──▶   E (Example) ──▶ P (Purpose) ──▶ I (Inspirational) ──▶ Consistent  ──▶
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1.  **E: They lead by example** by living the values and behaviours that they would expect from their followers (they walk the talk);
2.  **P: They lead with a purpose** – they understand and are passionately aligned to the organisation’s quest and higher purpose, and set the appropriate vision and supporting strategies;
3.  **I: They are inspirational** – people believe in them, what they do and what they represent, and want to follow them;
4.  **C: They are consistent** – leadership is not a part time activity, as leaders are under constant observation and scrutiny by their followers. They are therefore consistent in their behaviour, no matter where, when or whatever situation they are in, ensuring that all stakeholders are in constant receipt of relevant communication;

### Core Values & Behaviours

As a business leader the energy that you send into the world is critical. As you grow, the ability to create a team around you that has the same energy becomes an integral part of your organisation.

This energy derives from the centre of what you believe is important, and it can be expressed in terms of your core values (the beliefs on which the organisation is built). These align closely to your leadership approach and style. Core values help you share what is important to the organization, both to your team and to your customers.

We all have a set of personal values or beliefs that govern how we behave, and how we believe others should behave. In reality we don’t actually see these values in a person, we only see their behaviour. We all know examples of people who say one thing and do another – and how off-putting this can be...

Remember, it is not what you SAY that is important, it is what you DO that counts.

**Our core values are important, but our behaviour is critical!**
