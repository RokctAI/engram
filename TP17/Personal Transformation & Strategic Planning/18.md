Now that you have improved your personal relationship with money, and empowered yourself with good financial record-keeping you are ready to move to stage 3, Investment readiness. At some point in your business journey, you will need to access growth finance. Depending on the stage of growth and size of the business, this could be as little as R50,000 or as much as R5 million.

To prepare you for this, our **Investment Readiness Team** will work with you through a series of online webinars to understand the expectations and language of the lender/investor. This ranges from legal requirements, ensuring that all business transactions flow through a separate business bank account and that you have sound cash flow management (including a savings account). The ongoing support of your mentor will help to ensure that you have all the necessary requirements in place.
