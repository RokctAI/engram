### 7.1. People

**Human Resources:** "Without people there is no business" is a frequently made statement, and this is true! Every single business needs people – even highly automated ones that run on the Internet! It is people that make the decisions in the business, and usually people that design the products, provide the services and interact with the customers. Having the right people, caring for them and maximising their potential is an essential step in the success of an organisation. In South Africa especially, people need to be trained to be efficient and effective in order to compete with the rest of the world.

**Leadership:** People also refers to you and your skills, as the leader or senior person in your organization. Your Vision, skills and strategic direction will be integral to the success of your business.

---

### 7.2. Systems and Processes

**Operational & QMS:** Not everyone thinks of business success in terms of its ‘processes’ and quality management systems. But a business that wants to grow needs strong systems and methods – these are the building blocks to growth. So, whilst people in a business may come and go, it is the regular and repeated, efficient systems and processes that keep the business working.

A business wanting to expand must develop systems that can be replicated. This includes things such as job descriptions, production methods, sales systems, quality standards, even things like telephone etiquette and dress code. Systems help run the business and maintain quality standards.

**Technology & Communications:** Technology and IT is an increasingly important component of many businesses – often technology can reduce the cost of a product or service, making it more competitive and attractive to customers. IT, communications and databases are all parts of the process component.

---

### 7.3. Finance & Governance

**Financial Sustainability & Growth:** Financial performance hardly needs any explanation I would think. Financial performance is all about improving profit margins, cutting costs, increasing sales, managing cash flow. Any organisation, including non-profits must be masters of their financial performance, for the simple reality is that a business lives or dies on its financial results. Learning how to build a financially efficient and profitable business is critical to success.

**Legislation & Compliance:** A big part of effective financial management is sound financial administration, including legislative compliance and governance processes. Effective governance, and legislative compliance forms an important part of audit reports, and thorough admin in general is essential for any business, from an operational and governance perspective.

---

### 7.4. Customers

Without customers you have no business, so customers are without doubt a critical component of success. This is true even for a non-profit, NGO or even a Church. Customers bring money into the organisation – they can be customers for a product or service, or donors and supporters. Every business needs to serve their customers well. Every business needs their customer to keep coming back.

**Products & Services:** Are your products or services aligned to the market you have identified as having the most potential for your business? Are you continuing to innovate, improve and advance your offering in order to meet the needs of your customers?

**Sales & Marketing:** It's the goal of a business to continually find and keep customers. How effective is your sales and marketing? Do you have the tools you need to succeed in this area? Are you bringing in the sales you need to grow your market share? Without Sales, your business cannot survive.
