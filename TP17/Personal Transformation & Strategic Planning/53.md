### Which ones apply to me?

*   **All employers** should be registered for UIF (as a minimum) if they have staff working for them permanently.
*   All businesses must submit company Income tax returns

### How to apply

*   Income Tax registration automatically approved at registration with CIPC
*   UIF and Workman’s Compensation registrations must be sent to the Department of Labour.

### Support:

1.  Find a professional and qualified service provider to manage or guide or assist with these matters.
2.  Educate yourself - Understand the above and what this means for your business.
3.  SARS is available for queries / support **www.sars.gov.za** and toll free on **0800 00 72 77**.
4.  Visit bizportal **www.bizportal.gov.za** and create your own profile.
