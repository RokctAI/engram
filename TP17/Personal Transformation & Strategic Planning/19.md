## 6. Principle #6 Start with WHY

### 6.1. Purpose – The 'Why' Behind the Business

The most successful businesses also have a unique reason for being – a quest or higher purpose – the deeper reason 'Why' they exist. Ideally this is a 'why' that resonates with the founders and differentiates itself from other businesses. Do you know yours?

Understanding, living and sharing this reason 'Why' is one of the keys to building a sustainable business. It is this higher purpose that unites teams and incites passion and commitment from staff and clients alike. It is this higher purpose that sustains a business over decades.

Aligned to this 'Why', are other elements such as Vision (Where we are going) and Mission (What we do to get there) as well as Values (How we work together).

The definitions below and examples help to explain the differences:

*   **PURPOSE (your 'Why'):**
    The purpose statement of a business provides the reason or reasons your business exists. It answers the ultimate WHY your business exists.
    *   **NIKE:** *To use the power of sport to move the world forward.*

*   **VISION (your 'Where'):**
    A vision is a vivid mental image of the success you are wanting to create. It shares what this looks like or will look like in the future, often based on the founders’ personal goals and aspirations. Having a clear and inspiring vision will give the business a focus and keep it heading in the right direction. It answers the question WHERE you are heading.
    *   **NIKE:** *To bring inspiration and innovation to every athlete in the world.*

*   **MISSION (Your 'How/What'):**
    A Mission Statement describes what you do. It explains a bit about the products & services and how they are rolled out and may touch on things like geographical location and customers served as well. It answers the question WHAT do we do & HOW do we do it.
    *   **NIKE:** *Our mission is what drives us to do everything possible to expand human potential. We do that by creating ground-breaking sporting innovations, by making our products more sustainable, by building a creative and diverse global team and by making a positive impact in communities where we live and work.*

It is important to take some time on all of these elements when starting out or relooking at your strategic focus in the business. Understanding your higher purpose assists in formulating a lasting & meaningful vision statement and keeps us on track to achieve that vision. This is why your Purpose is usually a logical place to start.
