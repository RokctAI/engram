## 7. Principle #7 – Build a Solid Foundation

### The Five Pillars

As we learnt thus far, any organisation that wants to succeed should have a clear plan, which is reviewed every 6-12 months. Earlier, we created a strong Vision statement, and discussed some of goals and actions that are needed to succeed. But a business needs more than ideas and dreams, and actions only have relevance if they are appropriate and important. A business needs to know what is most important to its success.

This section is about identifying what areas are critical to master if we are to succeed. We call these the Five Pillars of success.

### What are the Five Pillars?

The Five Pillars (FP) are the components of the business that are crucial to its success. They are five essential areas that describe WHAT you do; what you absolutely have to get right. Think of the Five Pillars as the foundations of a house – they need to be solid if the house is to last!

These pillars are:
*   **People**
*   **Systems and Processes**
*   **Finance and Governance**
*   **Customers**
*   **Social and Environmental**
