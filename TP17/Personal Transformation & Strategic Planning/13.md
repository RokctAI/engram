## 4. Principle #4 – Personal Mastery Starts With Knowing Yourself

> Personal mastery is not something you possess. It is a process. It is a lifelong discipline.
>
> People with a high level of personal mastery live in a continual learning mode. They are acutely aware of their ignorance, their incompetence, their growth areas.
>
> Yet they are deeply self-confident, for they see that "the journey is the reward".

*   Personal Mastery investigates that age-old question of **"Who am I and what is my purpose?"**
*   Personal Mastery is vital in leadership - as we need to know and grow ourselves before we can know and grow others. There is a saying that 'we teach what we need to learn', and no more so than in this field.

### 4.1 Personal Mastery Model - The Seven Areas of Life

Seeing the centre of the wheel as 0 and the outer edges as 10.
On a scale of 1-10 rate your satisfaction levels of each area (0 being least satisfied and 10 being most satisfied).

The seven areas evaluated are:
1.  Vocation/Work
2.  Financial
3.  Mental
4.  Physical
5.  Spiritual
6.  Friends
7.  Family
