**Another example,** if your Strategic Objective says ‘Have our training course SETA certified’, a KPI that would help you attain this goal would be ‘Material aligned to SETA standards by 15 June’ or ‘Material submitted to the assessing body by 12 July’.

**One more:** If one of your Strategic Objectives is to ‘Expand into one new Territory’, your KPI could be ‘Sales rep in place in Gauteng by end August.’

These KPIs can be broken down into a few steps or left as one step that needs to be completed to realise the goal/ objective in your Strategic Action Plan.

KPIs are intended to help us take **positive steps** towards the achievement of our goals and objectives. They are a way to encourage and measure our progress, and can be structured in a strategic way, with the completion of each one taking us one step closer to the attainment of the goal in question.

Using the template provided, please write your own KPIs for the Strategic Objectives you listed in your Plan. Some Objectives may have more than one target, this is fine.

Once this step is complete, you will have completed your Business Plan on a Page – this is the start of your journey to a stronger, more focused and successful business!

### Choosing Key performance indicators

1.  What is the goal/ target (SMART goals are best)
2.  How will you recognize success?
3.  How will you measure (and report) it?
4.  What is the due date?

```
          What is the due date? ──▶ What is your goal?
                   ▲                     │
                   │                     ▼
         How will you measure it? ◀── How will you recognise
               (KPI)?                     success?
```

### Example

| :--- | :--- |

| **2. How will you know it has been achieved?** | Signed contract with approved water technician detailing remuneration, terms and expectations |
