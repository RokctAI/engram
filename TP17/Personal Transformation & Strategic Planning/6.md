# Business Readiness Checklist

This page contains checklists to assess business readiness in the following areas:

## 1. Leadership and Vision
*   Appropriate & Inspiring Vision Statement
*   Action Plan & clear set of Goals
*   SWOT Analysis
*   Full Business Plan

## 2. Product or service
*   Product or service is aligned to market needs
*   Pricing is appropriate & accepted by the market
*   Trademark and accreditations where necessary
*   Steady & uninterrupted access to sufficient products to sell and/or to sufficient capacity to deliver services
*   Product innovation and continual improvements

## 3. Operational and Quality Management Systems
*   Quality Management Systems in place
*   Company systems and processes are documented and available to the team
*   Systems are not dependent on specific individuals for their successful implementation
*   Systems make use of the appropriate technology available
*   Necessary safety & 3rd party liability protection is in place
*   Effective & efficient administration processes in place
