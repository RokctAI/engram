Building on the work done in the **Fetola Success Wheel Scorecard**, the **Business Plan on a Page** is the model that we use to identify the priorities and focus areas to create forward momentum. The **Plan on a Page** has the following elements:

*   **Vision** – what is the desired future goal of the business? The big destination?
*   **The Five Pillars** – what major elements need mastering in order to succeed?
*   **Strategic Objectives** – what core actions are needed to achieve this mastery?
*   **Key Performance Indicators (KPI's)** – How does a business measure its progress?

The purpose of this workshop is to get you started on a concise, action-oriented plan for your own and your company's future. The outcome of our work together over the next two days, will be a clear uncluttered document that we call the **'Plan on a Page'** (see Figure 1).

This Plan on a page will form the basis of our work together over the coming months.

This one-page summary of your business and its objectives aligns to the gaps identified in your **Fetola Success Wheel Scorecard** and will help you to create focus and direction in your organisation. A template is provided that you will complete during this workshop and refine further as the programme progresses.

#### **1. PEOPLE**
*   **Five Pillars:** People are the most important part of the business.
*   **Strategic Objectives (Building a team of people who can grow the business):**
    1. Boost sales team
    2. Improve HR management & recordkeeping
    3. Improve staff retention & team culture
    4. Train & upskill key staff
*   **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
    *   Interview & appoint new rep (Dec)
    *   JD's & contracts in place (Jan)
    *   Weekly team meetings (Jan)
    *   Training plan in place (Mar)

#### **2. PROCESSES**
*   **Five Pillars:** Systems drive our expansion & growth.
*   **Strategic Objectives (We manage this business with professional systems):**
    1. Streamline sales function
    2. Implement QMS
    3. Update IT & telecommunications
    4. Improve internal communications
*   **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
    *   One Customer list (Mar)
    *   QMS system in place (Apr)

#### **3. FINANCE**
*   **Five Pillars:** We are a profitable business - 10% NPAT.
*   **Strategic Objectives (We are a healthy, profitable business):**
    1. Increase GP margin by 5% by 2022
    2. Implement financial management system
    3. Develop in-house financial management skills
    4. Reduce overheads by 10% before next FYE
*   **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
    *   Monthly Sales target met (May)
    *   New pricelist in place (July)
    *   New bookkeeper (Feb)
    *   Office sub-let (Aug)

#### **4. CUSTOMERS**

    2. Improve customer retention rate
